# FitDrip - Never struggle with picking an outfit again.

Fitdrip is your digital wardrobe and outfit stylist. Upload photos of your apparel/accessories to save them into your wardrobe, create your outfits, or let the AI stylist to create outfits for you.

## How it works

**Upload your pieces**
Take a photo of an item and store it by type — tee, pants, hoodie, or shoe, accessory, etc

**Label the style**
Tag each piece with how it looks and fits: cropped, oversized, straight-legged, baggy, fitted, chunky, colour, etc

**Build a wardrobe**
Every piece you upload lives in your wardrobe, browsable and filterable.

**Ask the AI stylist**
Describe what you want and the chatbot puts together outfits from pieces you own — "cropped hoodie with baggy pants and chunky shoes." Save any outfit suggestion you like.

**Build your own outfits**
Mix and match pieces yourself, name the outfit, and save it.
